Welcome. Let's set up the house.

Four steps. The computer on the television, then the family's phones.

Not a medical device. Setup does not turn on emergency monitoring. Rammy does not call 911. In an emergency, call 911.

  1. 1

    Install Rammy on a PC

    Install Rammy on the Windows PC or Mac that is connected to the television. No special graphics card.

  2. 2

    Open the family setup page

    On that same computer, open the family setup page.

  3. 3

    Tap Invite family

    Tap Invite family. A QR code appears on the screen.

  4. 4

    Scan it with Rammy Alerts

    Open Rammy Alerts and scan that QR code. The phone app is how family joins the house.
